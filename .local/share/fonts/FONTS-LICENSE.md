The icon fonts used by the bar (`JarvisBarIcons`, `JarvisTaskbar`, `JarvisStart`,
`JarvisBongo`, `JarvisWave`, `WolfGlyph`, `ZenGlyph`) are not stored here: `install.sh`
builds them with the `build_*.py` scripts in `~/.config/hypr/scripts`.
